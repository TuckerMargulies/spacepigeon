SUBWAY TO OUTER SPACE

WE STARTED WITH HUMANS.

We set out to build the very best homosapien noggin shell in the solar system. But NASA said they had that covered. So we moved to the next best thing – our muse and neighbor, the intrepid pigeon. (1 million potential customers in NYC alone!).

If there is any being capable of exploring beyond our solar system its the Pigeon. Not convinced?

Reconsider the Pigeon.

1. BASICALLY SUPERHEROES

Pigeons can problem-solve, making intelligent choices, and categorize common items.

Scientists have taught them to identify the alphabet, do math, and play ping pong.

They have extra powerful wings and super lungs that help them retain more oxygen than most birds, allowing them to fly up to 100 MPH.

They have extraordinary vision. Pigeons photo sensors and light filters can differentiate as many as five spectral bands. They see the world as a Kaleidoscope.

2. SERIOUSLY HEROES

Carrier Pigeons helped start the news agency Reuters in the 1840's and carried World War I and World War 2.

32 war pigeons were awarded the Dickin Medal, an honor given to animals in the UK that have displayed "conspicuous gallantry or devotion to duty."

One of the most celebrated war pigeons was Cher Ami, a pigeon who arrived to her loft shot through the breast, blinded in one eye, and missing most of her leg, but carrying her message nonetheless.

She is credited with saving the the lives of 194 men from the American 77th Infantry Division in World War I. You can find her remains in the Smithsonian Institution today.

3. FAMOUS FAN CLUB

Nicola Tesla, Mike Tyson, and Pablo Picasso have all had Pigeon friend. Picasso named his daughter after them.
